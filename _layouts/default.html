OD85 FULLRANGE OVERDRIVE SHAPED FOR YOUR DAW

FREE DOWNLOAD : OD85 FULLRANGE OVERDRIVE VST3 PLUGIN 0.1.0

OD85 Fullrange is a versatile overdrive plug-in designed to cover a wide range of drive tones, from responsive edge-of-breakup sounds to heavier saturation.

Its input control shapes the signal before the drive stage, while the post-drive soft gate helps reduce noise as the sound decays.

Controls and features

DRIVE: Sets the amount of overdrive.

LEVEL: Adjusts the output volume.

TONE: Shapes the sound from darker to brighter.

INPUT: Adjusts the signal level entering the drive stage.

GATE: Reduce noise when the input signal falls below the gate setting.

STEREO: Toggles stereo processing.

2X: Enables 2x Oversampling for increased processing quality.

HI: Switches to a higher-gain voicing.

Effect ON/OFF: Toggle the pedal effect with the footswitch.

Dial in responsive gain, push the output, and use tone to explore different degrees of openness, compression, and harmonic character. Refine the signal with input filtering.
